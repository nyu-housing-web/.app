ABOUT NYU HOUSING

A simpler way to find a home near NYU.

NYU Off-Campus Housing is a demo housing experience designed to bring listings, agents, and useful housing tools together in one place.

WHAT WE DO

Housing search, made easier.

Finding a place in New York can mean comparing apartments, rooms, shared housing, locations, prices, and move-in timing. This experience puts those choices into one focused interface so you can explore options without jumping between different screens.

Browse available housing, narrow your search, save places you like, explore housing details, and connect with a landlord or housing agent when you find an option that interests you.
